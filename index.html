Welcome to Turning Pages

Features / About the Site

Turning Pages is designed to be simple and easy to navigate, connecting all pages through a clear structure.

  • Clear navigation linking Home, Discover, Reviews, Pages Turned, and Goals
  • Consistent color scheme and style across every page for a cohesive feel
  • Accessible navigation bar present on all pages for quick movement between sections
  • The Discover page also links to Reviews for a slightly deeper level of navigation

Future Additions

While the design stays consistent, each page will have a layout suited to its specific purpose.

  • Discover page for browsing and exploring new books
  • Pages Turned to organize and manage your reading lists
  • Goals page for tracking your reading progress
  • Each subpage designed for specific actions rather than general information
