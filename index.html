Features / About the Site

Turning Pages is a reading companion built around finding books, sharing opinions, and tracking your progress over time.

  • The Discover page helps you search by title, author, keyword, and genre so you can quickly find your next read
  • The Reviews page lets you save personal reviews with star ratings so your reactions are easy to revisit later
  • Discover and Reviews work together, so books you find can flow naturally into your saved review history
  • Navigation stays simple and consistent, making it easy to move between exploring, reviewing, and tracking

Future Additions

Future development will focus on turning Turning Pages into a connected reading community.

  • Integrate a database so reviews, progress, and reading activity are reliably stored and not limited to one browser
  • Add user accounts so each reader can sign in and maintain a personal profile, history, and preferences
  • Enable community review viewing so users can browse what other readers thought about the same books
  • Expand the platform from a personal tracker into a shared space for discovering books through other readers
